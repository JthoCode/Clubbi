Clubbi

Privacy.

Clubbi stores the information needed to operate school activities: names, email addresses, school membership, event signups, and feedback.

How information is used

We use account details to authenticate users, organize school participation, send event notifications, and respond to support requests. We do not sell student information.

School responsibility

Schools should only share the login phrase with their community and should contact Clubbi about correcting or removing information.

Questions

Use the feedback form to contact the beta team.
